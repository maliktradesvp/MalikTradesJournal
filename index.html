Trading journal

A trading journal you can keep

Log a trade below and everything here fills in: a P&L calendar, an equity curve, and the stats that tell you whether your edge is real. Nothing to set up, no account, free.

Tick the levels your entry was at and the setup grades itself — four or more confluences is an S, three an A, two a B, one a C. After a few dozen trades the grade table shows you whether your own A setups actually beat your C setups. That one table is the point of the whole thing.

Your trades are saved in this browser, on this device. They are never uploaded anywhere and nobody else can see them. Clearing your browsing data erases them, so hit Export backup now and then and keep the file somewhere safe.
