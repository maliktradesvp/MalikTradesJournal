A trading journal you can keep

Log a trade below and everything here fills in: your results in R, where your edge is by setup, level and session, what your mistakes cost, a P&L calendar and an equity curve.

Tick the confluences at your level and the trade rates itself, one star per confluence up to four. Add your stop size and every result is measured in R, so a win on MNQ and a win on GC compare fairly.

Your trades are saved in this browser, on this device. They're never uploaded anywhere and nobody else can see them. Clearing your browsing data erases them, so hit Backup now and then and keep the file somewhere safe.
